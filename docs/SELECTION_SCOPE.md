# GEO discovery and cohort-selection provenance

The original broad and strict Boolean GEO queries are in `geo_search/`; their results will depend on the GEO database snapshot and search time. Historical evidence indicates 1,150 distinct broad hits and 671 distinct strict hits. The manual/curated route from 671 strict records to 107 screening candidates was not fully programmatically logged. The file `00_metadata/frozen_search/HFD_primary_analysis_GSE_frozen_v1.txt` contains those 107 curated candidates, **not** the 64 GSEs in the final meta-analysis. The study-defined final 64 studies and 105 HFD-versus-control contrasts can be recovered from `00_metadata/analysis_inputs/frozen_v1/HFD_primary105_contrast_analysis_input_audit_v1_1.tsv`, and source selection may require manually documented review. Do not label the full trajectory automated or PRISMA-complete.
